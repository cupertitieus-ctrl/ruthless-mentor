Why I built this.

When I was writing my first chapter book, everyone told me it was amazing. Friends, family, everyone. And I believed them — of course I did. It was my first book. I had my author goggles on.

Then one guy I met in public told me my book had no plot. Not to be mean. Just honest. I had to rewrite the whole thing.

I wasn't mad at him. I was mad that I'd wasted months getting nothing but "it's great!" from everyone around me, just to have one stranger be the first person to tell me the truth.

That's why I built Ruthless Mentor. So you don't have to wait six months and a random encounter to find out what's actually wrong.

— Ryan

Questions.

My book rhymes. Will it try to turn it into couplets?

No. The review identifies your rhyme scheme first — AABB, ABCB, ABAB, or something less common — and every suggested fix comes back in the scheme you chose, matching your meter. If your form is unusual, it asks rather than assumes.

My manuscript is only 400 words. Is that too short?

That's the right length. 300–600 words is the target for a picture book, and short manuscripts get the same full report.

Will it complain that I didn't describe what things look like?

No. Picture book text is half the story — illustrations carry the rest. It won't ding you for leaving visuals to the illustrator. It will tell you where you've over-described something the art should handle.

Is this going to hurt my feelings?

Maybe. But every critique comes with a reason and a fix.

How do I submit?

Copy and paste your text directly into the submission form. You'll have the report in a few minutes, emailed to you as a shareable link.

Is my manuscript private?

Yes. Processed and gone. Nothing shared or trained on.

Can I re-submit after I revise?

Absolutely. Fix what got flagged, send it back, and the new review reflects the changes. Each submission is a new review at standard pricing.

Does this replace a critique group or an editor?

No. It gets your manuscript into strong shape first, so the humans you work with spend their time on the hard stuff instead of the obvious stuff. It doesn't do grammar or proofreading.
