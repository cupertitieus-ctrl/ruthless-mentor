Why this exists

Everyone told me my book was amazing.

When I was writing my first chapter book, everyone told me it was great. Friends, family, everyone. And I believed them — of course I did. It was my first book.

Then one guy I met in public told me it had no plot. Not to be mean. Just honest. I had to rewrite the whole thing.

I wasn't mad at him. I was mad that I'd spent months hearing "it's great!" from everyone around me, and a stranger was the first person to tell me the truth.

That's what this is. The stranger, on demand, before you've spent the money.

— Ryan

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Is this going to hurt my feelings?

Maybe. But every critique comes with a reason and a fix.

Does my draft need to be finished?

No. Rough drafts, single chapters, half-baked ideas — all welcome, and the earlier you send it the more time you save.

What happens to unused credits?

They roll over, up to the cap on your plan. Nothing expires while your plan is active.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Both, from your account, in one click. No email, no phone call. Credits you've already banked stay yours.

My book rhymes. Will it try to turn it into couplets?

No. Your rhyme scheme gets identified first — AABB, ABCB, ABAB, or something rarer — and every suggested fix comes back in the scheme you chose, matching your meter.

Is my manuscript private?

Yes. Processed and gone. Nothing shared, sold, or trained on.

Is this a replacement for an editor?

No. It gets your manuscript into strong shape first, so the humans you work with spend their time on the hard problems instead of the obvious ones. It doesn't do grammar or proofreading.
