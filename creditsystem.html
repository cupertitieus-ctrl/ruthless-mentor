Why I built this.

When I was writing my first chapter book, everyone told me it was amazing. Friends, family, everyone. And I believed them — of course I did. It was my first book. I had my author goggles on.

Then one guy I met in public told me my book had no plot. Not to be mean. Just honest. I had to rewrite the whole thing.

I wasn't mad at him. I was mad that I'd wasted months getting nothing but "it's great!" from everyone around me, just to have one stranger be the first person to tell me the truth.

That's why I built Ruthless Mentor. So you don't have to wait six months and a random encounter to find out what's actually wrong.

— Ryan

Questions.

Is this going to hurt my feelings?

Maybe. But every critique comes with a reason and a fix.

Does my draft need to be finished?

No. Rough drafts, single chapters, half-baked ideas. All welcome.

How do I submit?

Copy and paste your text directly into the submission form.

Is my stuff private?

Yes. Processed and gone. Nothing stored, shared, or trained on.

How does pricing work?

Three tiers: Children's/Chapter Book $15, Middle Grade/YA $20, Adult/Screenplay $25. Full review + printable report included.

Can I re-submit after I revise?

Absolutely. Fix the issues we flagged, submit again, and your new review will reflect the improvements. It's the cheapest way to track your progress as a writer. Each submission is a new review, so standard pricing applies — or use a subscription plan for monthly review credits.

Is this a replacement for an editor?

No. This is a tool that gets your manuscript into a strong, complete draft so when you do work with a developmental editor, you get the most out of it.

Does this do any copy/line editing or proofreading?

It does not catch grammar or punctuation. It is purely there to see if you have a story arc, if you developed your characters, etc. Please see our sample report above.
